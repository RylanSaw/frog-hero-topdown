FROG HERO ARENA

Defend the sacred lily pond! Move with the left stick or WASD, slash swamp beetles and slimes with your emerald leaf blade, and launch your sticky tongue to snatch golden fireflies or pull enemies in.

HERO ABILITIES & CONTROLS

FROG HERO GUIDE

  • Multi-Touch Movement: Drag the left virtual joystick with your left thumb while tapping SLASH or TONGUE with your right thumb at the same time.
  • Leaf Blade Slash (Space / Click): Wide melee arc that knocks back and defeats swamp slimes and armored beetles.
  • Sticky Tongue Snatch (E / Shift): Shoots forward to grab distant golden fireflies (+50 pts & +10 HP) or yank monsters straight into sword range.
  • Wave Progression: Defeat each wave of swamp invaders to unlock faster enemies and higher scores.
